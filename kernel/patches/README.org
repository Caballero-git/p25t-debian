#+TITLE: P25T kernel patches

* What this is
30 patches on the Linux 7.3 release (tag =v7.3=, commit
=c03c1a14507dfaf8b856c2608f9ac216021fc222=, 2026-10-18). They add the
Teclast P25T (P9A6, RK3566): its device tree, the panel, touchscreen,
cameras and a few driver fixes. =tools/get_kernel.sh= downloads 7.3 and
applies them; the first patch carries the =base-commit:= line.

They were written during the tablet's bring-up on a commit between
7.3-rc4 and rc5 and moved to 7.3 on 2026-10-22 without a conflict: none
of the 18 files they touch changed upstream in between, and the device
trees built from 7.3 are byte-identical to the earlier ones.

* Notes
- Some commit messages still give the numbers the patches had during the
  bring-up (e.g. "patch 0008"); the bring-up history is not public.
- The kernel makes four device trees: =rk3566-teclast-p25t.dtb= (USB-C
  as a device), =-usbhost= (USB-C as a host, e.g. a cable keyboard),
  =-front= and =-front-usbhost= (the same with the front camera instead
  of the rear one).
- The firmware note in 0017 names a bring-up tool that is not public;
  the firmware itself is in the patch.
